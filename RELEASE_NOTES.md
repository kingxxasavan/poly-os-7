PolyOS 1.0 is the first full release of PolyOS 7 for Debian.

- **Poly Account, if you want one.** Connect during setup or in Settings › Poly Account, or keep using PolyOS on its own. A new sign-in card: sign in, use a 6-digit code from the website, or create an account in one place.
- **Updates on your schedule.** Settings › Updates downloads in the background and installs tonight or at the time you choose, and asks before restarting.
- **Signed updates.** PolyOS checks Poly's signature and every file before it installs anything.
- **Poly Sync.** Your theme, wallpaper, taskbar and pinned apps, the same on every computer on your account.
- **Manage from the website.** See your computers, check for updates, and, with Remote management on, restart, lock or update them.
- **Dual boot, drivers and more** from 0.8: BitLocker help, installing on your own partition, touchscreen and camera drivers, and a hardware check that knows your computer's model.
