PolyOS 1.1.1 fixes waking up from sleep and a USB drive left plugged in.

- **Waking up works.** Closing the lid no longer leads to a black screen when you open it: PolyOS finishes drawing the lock screen before the computer sleeps, and redraws the screen when it wakes.
- **USB drive left in?** Its menu now starts the PolyOS installed on your computer after 5 seconds, instead of the installer. The USB entries are still there if you need them.
- **"PolyOS was updated".** After an update installs, automatically or not, PolyOS tells you once it's running the new version, with a link to what's new.
