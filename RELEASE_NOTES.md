PolyOS 1.1 makes installing one smooth step.

- **Set up once.** Everything is asked on the USB drive before installing: the hardware check, your edition, account, look, Wi-Fi and, if you like, a Poly Account. After the restart you go straight to your desktop, with no second setup.
- **Drivers and apps by themselves.** The recommended drivers and your edition's apps (Gaming or Developer) install in the background once you're online, with a notification when they're done.
- **Restarting after installing works.** Click Restart now with the USB drive still in; the computer starts PolyOS from its own drive, and you remove the USB once the screen goes dark. No more freezing on an error.
- **No recovery key during setup.** You can make one any time in Settings › Account.
- **Full screen is really full screen.** No bar pops up over full-screen apps; Win+F leaves full screen.
- **Smoother setup screens.** The logo and 7 no longer flicker while installing or downloading.
